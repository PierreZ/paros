# paros-coordinator

The three coordinators of `docs/architecture/04-roles.md`: the cell coordinator (`cell`, a term over the cell control journal), the tenant coordinator (`tenant`, each tenant's sets, moves, modes, roles and scrubs) and the universe coordinator (`universe`). Each is a client of the cell: it sits above `paros-client` and below `paros-machine`.

A layer of the library (#722 (three products)): it depends only on the layers below it (root *Layout*). Programs use it through the facade, `paros::coordinator`.

## Map

- `cell.rs` → `serve_term`, `TermDuty`, `Watch`, `election_tunables` → the cell coordinator (#240): every founding member campaigns in a task beside its node loop (seed and BUGGIFY decisions drawn on the loop: a stalled leader, a hand-off; a term-ending decision is a fault, never fired in the recovery tail, `chaos`); a won term installs its uuid on the cell control journal (only over the leader it read before it read the election again, `Client::claim_over`: a term the election moved past installs nothing, #653 (election term to installed leader)), folds it, finishes in-flight admissions and joins the cell to the universe (`universe.rs`), then publishes the interface, renewing whenever a renewal falls due while each duty runs (`renewing`; a slow install is a BUGGIFY decision, #542 (coordinator starves its lease while it installs)); a lost install resigns; a term that could not claim dials the founders where the registry holds them next (`follow_book`, a renamed founder, #529). For the rest of a served term its `Watch` (#211, D6) `Identify`s every founding member and registered machine at each renewal period and writes changes only: a new incarnation of a registered machine registers again, `MachineUp` for one held down or seen as another incarnation, `MachineDown` after `machine_down_after` of silence, a machine down and silent past `machine_replace_after` named gone outside every open maintenance window (`Watch::gone`, #475 (re-place after the Down bound), #359), `MachineAdmitted` once for a registered machine that answers with this cell's id (#511), `Admit` again with the cell's book and its fold's position to an admitted machine whose reported `registry_position` stopped below the coordinator's fold (`CellSession::readmit`, #505 (stranded admitted machine)), and it releases the slots of every tenant the cell dropped (#212 (placement)); the term's first write that does not land ends the watch (never fight an admin session's fence). A served term registers a `CoordinatorInterface` group (#404) serving `Register` (#349) and `JournalRequest`, publishes its reference with the address, and drops it when the term ends (a BUGGIFY decision keeps it one more step, answering `not_coordinator`): the term's coordinator `Identify`s the machine at the address it asks for and writes `RegisterNode` when the registry holds another address, name or machine interface. A served term answers `JournalRequest` as the tenant coordinator (`tenant.rs`, #210). `Audit::call_observer` hands a harness the coordinator's calls.
- `universe.rs` → `joining`, `tenant_steps`, `follow`, `directory_reader`, `join` → the cell's side of the universe (#513 (fleet Init duel), #225); the directory is read at the registry's universe pointer when another cell hosts it (`directory_reader`, #232 (multiple cells)), and `join` answers `JoinUniverse`: an idle cell's coordinator reads the directory at the request's pointer and writes `JoinFleet` with that pointer once the directory holds the cell; at its install and at each watch round the coordinator reads the universe directory; once it holds this cell and its control journal, the coordinator writes `JoinFleet` with the directory's fleet id, then, for each `users` tenant of the cell `REGISTERING` and neither hosted nor dropped, its birth footprint (`GrantCapacity`; a refusal only once every founder registered, #476 (tenant footprint)) and `HostTenant` once it is granted, and `DropTenant` for each one `REMOVING` and not dropped; `init` and a tenant's creation or removal wait for these writes and never claim the cell control journal.
- `tenant/modes.rs` → `Modes` → the tenant coordinator's mode changes (#214 (tenant modes)): one step per watch round, after the move step, in turn over the live journals whose recorded placement (`TenantJournal::placed`) is not their desired shape on exactly their slots (a journal with a slot on a draining machine is the move's): book the slots the mode lacks (a `hint!` after it), reconfigure the leader onto the new members under the new quorum system (a smaller mode keeps the slots placement does not release), then, once in force, record `Placed` and, once the leader's GC watermark reached it, release the slots of the members it left (it retires none: a later mode may place the journal on that machine again); a later term finishes a change an earlier one started.
- `tenant/sets.rs` → `Sets`, `SET`, `set_of` → the tenant coordinator's matchmaker sets (#501 (matchmaker sets off the founders)), sized `2F+1` from the tenant's largest `failures_per_partition` (at least 1, at most every storage machine placement may book, #214 (tenant modes)): one step per watch round, in turn over the hosted tenants but the cell's: book the slots a set lacks with `paros::model::placement` (`Role::Matchmaker`; a set born on the founders holds none, so it is placed the first time), hand it over onto the booked machines that stay through a founding member (`ReconfigureMatchmakers` names its tenant), then release every slot on a draining machine once the set in force (the founders' latest belief for the tenant's control journal) is those machines (a `hint!` after a booking; the first term that advances a move may resign, the same BUGGIFY decision as a journal move's). With none pending, a settled set is judged again in turn: a node that finishes a frozen generation proposes the members that answered its freeze, so a set can leave its booked machines after its move; a term's first finished move asks its handover again on a BUGGIFY decision of the candidacy (a late retry). A set with a slot on a gone machine and no machine to take it is handed over onto its staying machines alone (a thinner generation), never back onto the gone one: a wiped machine never answers its bootstrap, the frozen generation refuses GC, and every move of the tenant waits on that watermark (witness recipe in `Sets::step`).
- `tenant.rs` → `TenantDesk` → the tenant coordinator for one term (#210): claims each tenant control journal under the term uuid, catches the cell's fold up at every request so a dropped tenant is refused by every term (#395), writes `Describe` once, answers a request from its recorded outcome or writes it (id drawn from the candidacy's seed; a reused id on a BUGGIFY decision of the node loop). A create is placed over the cell's storage machines, founding or admitted, with `paros::model::placement` (#474), and its acceptor slots are booked through the term's cell session before it is written; a create with no slot is recorded `Unplaceable`; a delete's slots are released after its tombstone, and slots no live journal holds by the next create in the tenant; a term's first create is abandoned after its bookings on a BUGGIFY decision (#212 (placement)). A write past the fold is caught up, and a catch-up that falls short answers `unavailable` (#445 (ignored load result)); an impatient term, a BUGGIFY decision of the node loop, stops listening at once on its first create and its first catch-up.
- `cell/capacity.rs` → `grant` → the cell coordinator's answer to a `CapacityRequest` (#476 (tenant footprint)): a decided id answered from its record, a tenant the cell neither hosts nor booked refused `unknown_tenant`, else the grant (ids drawn from the term's desk) or its refusal written in the cell control journal; the tenant desk books a create's acceptors through it.
- `tenant/scrub.rs` → `Scrubs`, `ScrubPace`, `PAGES_PER_STEP`, `FLUSH_PAGES` → the tenant scrubber (#362 (background scrubbing), §5): one step per watch round, after the footprint step, in turn over the hosted tenants: up to `PAGES_PER_STEP` pages of the tenant's pass over its live journals in id order, each page asked of every machine with an acceptor slot of the journal (never a quorum read), judged (a page ends at the lowest next slot answered; one slot under one ballot with two fingerprints is a mismatch), paced by a token bucket of `scrub_rate` and the `scrub_pass_within` bound, and flushed to the tenant control journal (`TenantCommand::Scrubbed`) every `FLUSH_PAGES` pages, at a journal's end and on damage; gate "scrub: the scrubber found and repaired a record no client read".
- `tenant/roles.rs` → `Roles`, `RoleStep` → the tenant coordinator's role counts (#604 (role counts), §3.4): one step per watch round, after the footprint step, in turn over the hosted tenants and over `PLACED` (`Role::Batcher`, `Role::Frontend`, #723 (frontend as a parosd role)): release a slot of the role on a machine that leaves, else book or release `Role::Batcher` slots of the tenant (`BookingTarget::Tenant`) with `paros::model::placement` until it holds `min(batchers, stateless machines that can take one)`.
- `cell/footprints.rs` → `Footprints` → one footprint step per watch round, after the set step, in turn over the tenants with a granted footprint (#476 (tenant footprint)): `paros::model::footprint::fix`, written through the term's cell session; first, the cell tenant's and the universe tenant's footprints, which `init` never books (never refused).
- `cell.rs` (#360 (disk full)) → `Watch::judge_space`: each round, a machine whose `Load` answer reports free bytes below `free_bytes_floor` is written `MachineSpace` (changes only); placement books it last (`Registry::is_low_on_space`, `Liveness::low_space`).
- `cell/slow.rs` → `SlowJudge`, `SlowBounds`, `Sample`, `Judged` → slow machines (#361 (slow machines)): the watch's facts per round (`Identify` round trip, the `Load` window's disk and CPU busy ratios) judged with hysteresis (`slow_rtt` and four times the round's median, `slow_busy_permille`, `slow_rounds` rounds in a row), written `MachineSlow`; at most a minority of the pool held slow (`cap`). Pure.
- `tenant/moves.rs` → `Moves` → the tenant coordinator's moves off draining machines and slow ones (`Registry::keeps_roles`, #361 (slow machines)) (#474 (journals on admitted machines)): one step per watch round, in turn over the journals with a slot on a draining machine: book a slot elsewhere (a `hint!` after it), reconfigure the journal's leader onto the machines that stay, then, once the leader's GC watermark reached the configuration in force, record it as `Placed` (#361), retire the old member if the leader's floor names it and release its slot; a later term finishes a move an earlier one started (the first term that advances one resigns on a BUGGIFY decision of the candidacy). A machine gone (#475) moves like a draining one; a move that would book a slot its desired mode releases keeps the staying members and records the shrunk configuration (#633); a candidacy defers a re-placement once on a BUGGIFY decision.
- `cell/answers.rs` → what a served term answers: the machines' `Register` (#349) and the journal requests (#210).
- `cell/campaign.rs` → a founding member's candidacy (#240): the election loop, the terms it serves, the hand-on and the renewals.
- `cell/watch.rs` → `Watch` → the cell coordinator's failure detector over the cell's machines (#211) and the `Identify` probes of a machine's new address.
